Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Data controller

The data controller is the operator of Verdi Colline B&B, Via Santa Lucia 45, Serra de’ Conti (Marche, Italy), reachable at +39 329 266 1135.

Data processed by this website

This is a static, informational website: it does not require registration and does not store data entered by visitors on its own servers. The contact form does not send data to the website: on submission, the text is simply opened in the visitor’s WhatsApp application, and the visitor decides whether to send it.

The hosting provider may collect technical logs (e.g. IP address, date and time of the request, browser type) required to deliver the service and ensure security, according to its own privacy policy.

Contact by phone and WhatsApp

When you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, the data you provide (name, contact details, dates of stay, requests) is used solely to answer your request and manage any booking. WhatsApp is a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.; the processing carried out by WhatsApp is governed by its own privacy policy.

Legal basis and retention

Data provided for information requests or bookings is processed to carry out pre-contractual and contractual measures requested by you and to comply with legal obligations applicable to accommodation providers. Data is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and meet regulatory obligations.

Your rights

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) you may request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing, object to processing and lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante). To exercise your rights, contact us using the details above.

Third-party services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts; the “Open in Google Maps” link leads to Google’s website. Use of these services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Note

This policy describes how the website currently works and will be updated if changes are made (for example the activation of analytics or online booking tools).
