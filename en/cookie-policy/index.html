Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store on the visitor’s device. Similar technologies (such as browser local storage) perform comparable functions.

Cookies used by this website

This website does not use profiling cookies or third-party tracking tools. The only data stored on your device is the choice made in the cookie banner (saved in the browser’s local storage under the key “vc-consent”), which is needed to remember your preference.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts: the request to Google’s server may transmit your IP address, but no cookies are set by this website.

Non-essential cookies

No statistics or marketing cookies are currently active. Should they be introduced in the future (for example traffic analytics tools), they will only be activated after your explicit consent through the banner, and this page will be updated.

Managing your preferences

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by clearing the website data in your browser or by using your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
