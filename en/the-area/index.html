The area

Discover the Marche

Serra de’ Conti is an ideal starting point to explore the sea, villages and nature of the region.

Senigallia – Velvet Beach

The long sandy beach on the Adriatic coast, with the Rotonda a Mare and the historic centre.

Castles of Jesi

Walled villages among the Verdicchio hills, with wineries, squares and views.

Frasassi Caves

One of the most spectacular cave systems in Europe, in the heart of the Gola della Rossa.

Conero Riviera

White cliffs, bays and clear sea at the foot of Monte Conero.
